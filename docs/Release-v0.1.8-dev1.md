Automatic match replays are available for Local VS and online matches.

- Match recordings save in `replays/` beside the launcher.
- The launcher's **Replays** tab lists players, fighters, stage and duration.
- **Watch replay** plays both fighters' recorded inputs without connecting online.
- **Space** pauses, **Tab** fast-forwards and **Esc** exits playback.
- Custom palettes are preserved in recordings. Interrupted matches are labelled partial.
- Replays require the same combat build and BIOS. Settings, saves and replay files are preserved by updates.

Training is excluded. ROMs, BIOS images and personal recordings are not included in the portable.
