# Planned Team Battle mode

Requested for a later update.

- Each player selects an ordered team of three mobile suits.
- A defeated suit is eliminated. That player's next suit enters the following round.
- The winning suit stays in play and recovers health and PS based on the remaining timer.
- Berserk availability refreshes between rounds.
- A player wins the match by eliminating all three opposing suits.

The exact timer-based recovery formula needs to be defined before implementation.
