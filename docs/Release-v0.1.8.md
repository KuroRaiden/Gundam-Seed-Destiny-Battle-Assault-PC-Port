- **Match replays:** Local VS and online matches save automatically in `replays/`. Browse and watch them from the launcher's Replays tab.
- **Training snapshots:** F7 saves a clean PNG with the stage and HUD removed, a blue background, and any enabled F5 frame meter or F6 collision overlays. Fixed captures during normal Training play.
- **Movelist:** F1 now includes Guard Cancel inputs.
- **Launcher themes:** Choose Default, Earth Alliance or white ZAFT, with faction emblems and the SEED Destiny logo. Improved sidebar centering and content spacing.
- **GBA color modes:** Choose Original colors, frontlit/unlit GBA LCD, backlit GBA SP or Classic GBA under Play > Battle display. The setting saves automatically and only changes your local display online.

Extract the complete portable ZIP to a new folder. Supply your own US ROM, Japanese ROM and GBA BIOS; none are included.

**Upgrading from 0.1.7 or older:** the older updater does not support the new Themes folder. Copy your personal settings files from `config/`, saves, `gamefiles/`, and any `replays/` or `snapshots/` into the new portable. Keep the new `config/Update-Portable.ps1` and `config/portable-manifest.json`. The included updater preserves your settings on future updates.

Replays require the game build and BIOS used to record them. Development-build replays still require their original development build.
